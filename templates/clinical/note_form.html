SOAP Note Guide
Subjective:

Patient's description of symptoms, history of present illness, chief complaint.

Objective:

Measurable data: vital signs, physical exam findings, lab results.

Assessment:

Diagnosis, differential diagnosis, problem list.

Plan:

Treatment plan, medications, follow-up schedule, referrals.

Tip: Use clear, concise language. Document all relevant findings.
